Badminton math

A club night dies in three places: a hall nobody measured, a round robin nobody timed, and a 29-29 nobody can think through. Three small calculators for exactly those moments - exact arithmetic, with every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Court planner - hall width and length in, full-size courts out: best orientation, clearances, and the spare margin.
  • Club night round robin - headcount, match length and courts available become matches, rounds and honest wall-clock time.
  • Rally score helper - any score becomes points-to-win for each side and the correct service box.

Labeled norms

Playing court 13.40 × 6.10 m (BWF, doubles). Clearances of 1.2 m beside and 1.5 m behind are common club norms, not BWF rules - check your venue. Rally scoring: first to 21, win by 2, cap 30. These are planning aids, not officiating rulings.
